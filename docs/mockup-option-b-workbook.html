📘
About the story
Concept

Papa's Spectacles is a short story from your Grade 5 English textbook Santoor. It is about a family who search for Papa's missing glasses.

The story shows how a loving family works together to solve a small, funny problem. The search takes everyone to different parts of the house — and the ending is a big surprise!

Main characters: Papa, Mama, and their children. The narrator of the story is one of the children.

Setting: A family home — living room, kitchen, garden.

📖
Story extract — read this carefully
Story

Here is how the story begins. Read it slowly and picture the scene in your mind:

"Papa could not find his spectacles anywhere. He looked on the table, under the sofa, and even inside the refrigerator — but they were nowhere to be found!

'I am sure I left them right here,' said Papa, becoming more and more frantic with every passing minute."

The whole family then joins the search. Mama checks the kitchen. The children run to the garden. After a long and funny search, little Ria looks up — and sees Papa's spectacles sitting right on top of his own head!

New vocabulary from the story
Words

Learn these words — they will appear in exam questions too.

WordMeaningIn a sentence
spectaclesGlasses worn to help you see clearlyPapa wore spectacles to read the newspaper.
misplacedPut in the wrong place; temporarily lostShe had misplaced her pencil again.
franticVery worried and rushing aboutThe dog was frantic when it heard thunder.
relievedHappy because a worry is overEveryone was relieved when the spectacles were found.
🧩
How to answer story questions
Step-by-step

When your teacher asks "What is the main idea of the story?", use this method:

1
Read the question carefully. Underline the key word — here it is "main idea".
2
Think about the whole story, not just one event. What is the biggest message or point?
3
Write a full sentence that names the characters and describes what they did or learned.
4
Check your answer: Does it cover the beginning, middle, and end? Is it one clear sentence?
⚠️
Common mistake to avoid
Warning
⚠️
❌ Weak answer: "The story is about spectacles." — This is too short and does not explain anything.

✅ Better answer: "The story is about a family who search everywhere for Papa's missing spectacles and discover them on top of his head."

Always include who the story is about, what they did, and how it ended.
✅
Quick check
Practice
📝 Answer in 1–2 sentences
Why does everyone in the family help Papa search for his spectacles? What does this tell you about their family?
