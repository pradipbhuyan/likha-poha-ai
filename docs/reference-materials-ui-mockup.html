Why does a heavier object not fall faster than a lighter one?
Science · Grade 9
This is because gravity applies a proportionally greater force on heavier objects, but their greater mass also means more inertia. Both effects cancel exactly — giving all objects the same acceleration (g = 9.8 m/s²) in the absence of air resistance. This was demonstrated by Galileo's famous experiment at the Leaning Tower of Pisa...
