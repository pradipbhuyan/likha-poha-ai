NLG INTERNAL AUDIT DELIVERY REVIEW CHECKLIST - NON AGILE
CH-197 Version 2.3
Complete reference for all 81 checklist items (S.No 1-81)

FORMAT: S.No | Title | Question | Look For

=== SECTION A: Project Initiation and Planning (One-Time Activity) ===

1 | MSA/SOW Availability | Does the project have a signed and valid SOW and MSA, or approval for uncontracted work? | SOW/MSA signatures and validity dates. If expired or missing, look for uncontracted work approval or Work Ahead Request.

2 | Agreed Acceptance Criteria | Has DONE/Acceptance criteria been defined and agreed with the customer? | Acceptance criteria in SOW/MSA, or email/MoM evidences, or criteria defined in Software Project Plan (SPP).

3 | Contract Review & Compliance | Has the PM reviewed the contract, raised alerts, and recorded compliance clauses in the Contract Compliance Tracker? | SOW/MSA contractual requirements (ODC, HIPAA, SOC). Alerts raised by PM. Estimation sheet review evidence. All clauses in Contract Compliance Tracker.

4 | Project Scope Definition | Is the project scope well-defined with clarity for execution? | SOW scope description. Clarity for project type (Development/Migration/Maintenance). What defines project success.

5 | Kick-Off Action Items | Are all kick-off actions tracked and closed with stakeholder commitment? | Kick-off Presentation, invitees, Meeting Results, Actions identified, open actions past target date.

6 | Estimation | Is the estimation model aligned with the applicable process model (Agile/Non-Agile)? | Estimation model used and customization. Estimating guideline. Appropriateness for process model (V-Process, Agile, Testing).

7 | Quality Assurance & Control | Do the estimation tasks cover QA, SQA reviews, testing, and rework time? | Tasks in approved estimation sheet. Reviews (SQA, peer), testing tasks, rework time inclusion.

8 | Schedule | Does the project schedule include all tasks, dependencies, and align with the estimation? | Project schedule (MS Project/Excel). Alignment between estimation and schedule. Variations to be assessed.

9 | Operational Process | Is the operational process defined in the SPP with tailoring and customization? | Approved SPP. Operational Process section. CM Plan, Communication Plan, Risk Management Plan, Staffing Plan, BCP, Metrics chosen.

10 | Testing Strategies | Has the project defined a testing strategy and process for all test types? | Test Strategy/Test Plan. Processes for unit testing, system testing, integration testing, defect capture, re-test, reporting.

11 | Environment Issues / Challenges | Are there challenges setting up dev/test/DevOps environments? | SOW/Schedule timelines for environment setup. Deviations and impact on schedule.

12 | Communication Plan | Are meetings planned at all levels adequate for governance? | SPP meetings planned (team meeting, DM meeting, GDE meeting, CDE meeting). Adequacy assessment.

13 | Preparation Of Cost Model | Is the team in place in line with the cost model? | Cost model team structure vs staffing plan. Deviations assessed.

14 | SQA Identification | Has an SQA been identified to carry out independent review of deliverables? | SQA in cost model or SOW. SQA tasks in estimation sheet.

15 | Performance Objectives / Metrics / Customer Goals | Are performance metrics defined using the more stringent of org or customer goals? | Metrics/goals defined. Org goals vs customer goals (use whichever is stricter). Sub-process management.

16 | Interested Parties | Have all internal and external interested parties been identified and registered? | Interested Parties Register. Internal and external stakeholders identified. Completeness and fulfilment requirements.

17 | Transition Phase | Are transition phase deliverables defined in the SPP in line with SOW? | Approved SPP and SOW. Transition deliverable list comparison. Highlight discrepancies.

18 | Steady State | Are steady state deliverables defined in the SPP in line with SOW? | Approved SPP and SOW. Steady state deliverable list comparison. Highlight discrepancies.

19 | SLA Agreement | Have SLAs/KPIs been defined and agreed with the customer before steady state? | SLAs/KPIs agreed with customer. Coverage of scope. Internally defined SLAs if customer does not require them.

=== SECTION B: Project Execution (Recurring / Regular Activity) ===

20 | Entry & Exit Criteria | Is the operational process with entry/exit criteria implemented effectively? | SPP execution model. Entry and Exit Criteria defined. Evidences of process being followed.

21 | Client Feedback | Is customer feedback collected at the defined frequency? | Frequency defined in SPP. Feedback received. Actions identified and implementation effectiveness. Non-Agile = at least once a year.

22 | Identification Of Risk/Opportunities | Are risks and opportunities identified, monitored, and tracked for closure? | Risk Register. Risk Priorities. Closure actions. Opportunity identification and how PM is leveraging them.

23 | Potential Risk Identification | Do you foresee potential risks the PM should work on? Any opportunity for growth, AI implementation, or expansion? | Auditor's own assessment. Scope, process model, project type, cost model, team structure, progress, security. THIS REQUIRES AUDITOR'S OWN ANALYSIS.

24 | Customer & Deliverable Sign Off Available | Are deliverables signed off by the customer and CCT updated? | Deliverable sign-off evidences. Deemed acceptance clause in SOW. CCT updates. Sign-offs for requirement/design changes.

25 | Actual GM Verification | Is Gross Margin variance tracked and reported in Accelerate/WSR? | Budget tracker. GM variance. Accelerate updates. Weekly Status Report.

26 | Accelerate Reporting | Do weekly status reports accurately reflect actual project status? | Weekly Status Report. Customer Status Report. Risks, issues, current outages reflected accurately.

27 | Meeting Results | Are MoMs maintained for all meetings per the defined frequency? | Meetings defined in SPP. Meeting results/MoMs. Actions tracked to closure.

28 | Cost Model Deviation | Can the current team meet project objectives if different from the cost model? | Cost model vs actual team. Staffing plan. Skill gaps. Training plan. Capability assessment.

29 | Induction On Process Execution | Have all team members been inducted on the execution process? | Induction process in SPP. Induction evidences for all team members.

30 | Skill Gap Analysis | Does the training tracker capture skill gaps and track trainings to closure? | Training tracker. Skill gaps identified. Trainings provided. Effectiveness. ESD awareness and advanced quality tools for TL domain projects.

31 | Handover/Takeover | Are team exits tracked and RRs raised in advance for smooth handover? | Staffing plan. Resource Requests for exits. Dates raised vs notice period. Sufficient time for handover/takeover.

32 | Reporting Issues | Are issues identified, tracked for closure, and reported weekly? | Issue management process in SPP. Issue logs for PM and technical issues. Closure tracking.

33 | Impact Analysis | Do change requests contain impact analysis and are they tracked in the CR log? | Change requests. Impact analysis (efforts and timelines). Change Request Log maintained.

34 | Vendor Evaluation | Are vendors evaluated for subcontracting and compliance tracked? | Subcontracting process. Vendor evaluations. Compliance clauses in CCT. Mark Not Applicable if no vendor involved.

35 | Additional Checklist | Are there additional checklists the project should be using? | Checklists defined for the project. Additional checklists for defect-less delivery (code review, deployment checklist, etc.).

36 | Project Goals / Customer KPI / NTT Goals | Are project goals aligned with KPIs/NTT goals and managed at sub-process level? | Performance objectives/Metrics. Org vs customer goals (stricter applies). Sub-process management. Quantitative Project Management.

37 | Deviation/Variation Causal Analysis | Are there variances requiring RCA and corrective actions? | Project Metrics Report. Variances from goals. RCA carried out. Corrective actions taken. Benefits realized.

38 | Regression Test | Has regression testing been carried out before releases/deployments? | Regression test packs. Updated and current status. Evidences of regression tests for each release.

39 | Addressing Defects | Are defects tracked to closure before delivery to the customer? | Defect tracking tool. Internal defects from peer review, lead review, SME review, SQA, testing. All phases (requirements, design, coding, testing).

40 | Corrective Action | Is the project conducting RCA for defects and implementing corrective actions? | RCA report. Types of defects analyzed. Defects missed from analysis. Corrective actions and implementation effectiveness.

41 | Testing Methodologies | Do the tests planned and executed meet customer requirements per SOW? | SOW tests required. Evidences of tests done. Deviations highlighted. Additional tests if needed.

42 | Test Case Scenario | Do test cases cover all scenarios with traceability? | Test cases prepared. Coverage for all tests. Traceability matrix. Variances highlighted.

43 | RCA Suggestion To PM | Where should RCA have been carried out that the project missed? | Auditor's own assessment. Risks, issues, customer complaints, metrics variances, defects, DR findings, GM variance, skill gaps. THIS REQUIRES AUDITOR'S OWN ANALYSIS.

44 | Configuration Area/Archival/SharePoint | Are all latest versions of configurable items in the configuration repository? | Configuration area/repository. Latest versions. Checked-out items and durations. Completeness of items.

45 | Configuration Audit | Are configuration audits conducted at defined frequency with findings closed timely? | Configuration audit reports. Frequency alignment. Timely closure of audit findings.

46 | Customer Escalation | Has the project taken corrective action for all customer escalations and feedback? | Customer feedback from all sources (NPS, CSAT, VONC, meetings). RCA carried out. Actions implemented. Meeting result actions.

47 | Challenges In Meeting Objectives & Project Goals | Are there challenges in meeting the performance goals/metrics? | Risks, issues, metrics variances, defects, DR findings, GM variance, skill gaps. Assess challenges not yet identified by the project.

48 | Previous NC | Have previous review findings been closed with corrective actions? | All Review/Audit findings in AMS. Open findings. Corrective actions for correctness and appropriateness.

49 | Decision Analysis Report | Has the project carried out DAR for all qualifying decision scenarios? | Multiple-solution scenarios. DAR conducted and results recorded in NCoRe format. Shared with customer as applicable.

50 | Medical Devices/Telecom Project | Is software for medical devices/telecom validated initially and after changes? APPLICABLE ONLY for Medical Devices/Telecom projects. | Validation techniques adopted. Changes incorporated adequately.

51 | Tele Communication Projects | Are TL measurement metrics defined, monitored, and reported? | SPP for TL metrics. Monthly data shared with Quality team. Analysis performed.

52 | N-KMP Submission | Have knowledge assets, best practices, and lessons learned been submitted to N-KMP? | N-KMP submitted assets. Reuse declarations. CSI/customer success stories around automation and Gen AI. Effort savings declared.

53 | BCP Training & Drill | Has BCP/BRP training been provided and drills conducted? | Business Resumption Plan, Disaster Recovery Plan, Business Continuity Plan. Drill evidences and results.

54 | SLA Monitoring and Slippage RCA | Is the project meeting SLAs/KPIs and sharing RCA for breaches? Has any penalty been paid? | SLA/KPI performance data. RCA Reports for breaches. RCA shared with customer. Penalty status.

55 | RCA Reports For Ticket With Slippage | Are RCAs carried out for individual tickets that missed SLA even if overall SLA is met? | SLA performance at individual ticket level. RCA for each slipped ticket even when overall SLA target is met.

56 | Capacity & Availability | Is capacity and availability tracked weekly in line with the plan? | Capacity and Availability Plan. Planned vs actual. Variances highlighted.

57 | Deployment Issue | Have all releases been deployed successfully with no issues? | Release plan provided by customer. Successful completion of all releases. Post-deployment issues if any.

58 | Continuous Service Improvement | Are automations, innovations, and CSIs implemented and reported in MBR/QBRs? | Service Improvement Plan. Improvements shown to customer. Frequency and forum for showcasing CSIs.

59 | Knowledge Articles & Knowledge Repository | Is the knowledge repository (KEDB) being maintained within the team? | KEDB/Knowledge Repository. Documents updated and current. Accessibility for team members.

60 | Service Catalogue | Is the service catalogue updated for all services per the latest SOW? | Service Catalogue. All services documented with relevant details. Alignment with current SOW scope.

61 | Generic | Additional finding not covered in any other checklist item. | Only use if a finding or observation from the transcript genuinely does not fit into any other S.No.

62 | Schedule Tracker | Is the project schedule tracked for dependencies, change requests, and kept up to date? | Project schedule (MS Project or equivalent). Dependencies tracked. Change requests reflected. Current and updated.

63 | Traceability Matrix | Is the traceability matrix updated for all change requests? | Requirements Traceability Matrix. Updates for all change requests. End-to-end traceability maintained.

64 | Non-Functional Requirements | Are functional and non-functional requirements adequately captured? | Software Requirements Specifications / Functional Specifications. Functional and non-functional requirements. Requirement allocation among resources.

65 | Design and Development | Are design documents available, reviewed, and approved? | Design document. Design constraints, testing requirements, resources, safety, environment, business, user, quality, functional, reliability all considered.

66 | Attrition | Is attrition in line with the organization-defined percentage? Is there delivery impact? | Attrition percentage for the project. Impact on delivery based on resource replacements vs cost model.

67 | Transition Tracker | Is the transition schedule tracked for dependencies, CRs, deliverables, and up to date? | Transition Project Schedule. Dependencies and change requests tracked. Deliverables tracked. Schedule current and updated.

68 | Transition Risk/Issue | Are there risks/issues with transition progress for successful completion? | SOW transition scope. Risk/Issue Register/Log. Transition progress. Risks or issues for successful completion.

69 | Stabilization Phase | Has stabilization been completed successfully with SLAs defined for steady state? | Current performance vs SLAs agreed with customer. Variance assessment. Appropriateness of SLAs agreed.

70 | Problem Management | Are problems identified, reported to customer, and tracked with ticket reduction RCA? | SOW for Problem Management scope. Problems identified and reported. Problem tracking and ageing. RCA and ticket reduction benefits.

71 | RCA For Internal Defects | Is RCA carried out for defects from peer review, SQA, internal testing, and customer-reported? | RCA report for all defect categories. Types analyzed. Missed defects. Corrective actions and implementation effectiveness.

72 | Productivity Improvement | For projects more than 2 years old, is productivity improvement observed? Mark Not Applicable for projects under 2 years. | Productivity and utilization values/trends in the MMR tool.

73 | Technical Accelerators Adoption | Has the project explored technical accelerators or tools to enhance delivery? | KMP, Tools Catalog. Tools like Axet, Co-pilot, etc. explored or adopted to speed up delivery.

74 | Service Expansion Opportunities | Has the team identified transformation, migration, or complementing service opportunities? | CSI Tracker. RiMAC opportunities captured. Transformation or expansion proposals.

75 | Gen AI Awareness | Have technical team members completed Gen AI trainings (Gen AI 101 mandatory, White Belt Level 1&2, Yellow Belt Level 3)? | CATALYS training records. Completion status. Gen AI tools/frameworks awareness sessions conducted.

76 | Gen AI Customer Proposal | Is the team identifying and proposing Gen AI implementation opportunities to the customer? | RCA reports for automation opportunities. Customer proposals made. Engagement on Gen AI topics.

77 | Gen AI Implementation | If client expressed interest in Gen AI, has the team met expectations and measured productivity improvement? | CSI Tracker. Customer feedback. Action plan for implementation. Productivity improvement measured.

=== SECTION C: Project Closure (One-Time Activity) ===

78 | Closure Audit | Has the project completed the closure audit with all required documents and metrics updated? | Customer sign-off evidence. Closure meeting with stakeholders. Published/updated closure report.

79 | N-KMP Closure Submission | Have knowledge assets been submitted to N-KMP at closure? | N-KMP submitted assets. Reuse declarations for the project.

80 | Closure Archival | Have all project documents been uploaded to the organization archival repository? | Organization archival repository. All artifacts including reusable components uploaded.

81 | Case Study Submission | Has the case study been prepared and submitted to the organizational repository? | Case study on the engagement. Project video on achievements. Organizational Case Study repository.
