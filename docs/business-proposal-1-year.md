# Likha Poha AI Tutor: 1-Year Business Proposal

## Objective

Build a paid parent subscription business for CBSE + SOF AI tutoring with configurable plans, RAG-based SOF content, AI doubt solving, mock tests, parent visibility, and controlled AI usage costs.

All figures below are in INR, GST/taxes excluded, and assume steady paid subscriber counts across the year.

## Commercial Assumptions

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---:|
| Standard plan price | Rs. 499/month |
| Premium SOF plan price | Rs. 999/month |
| Family Premium plan price | Rs. 1,499/month |
| Assumed paid plan mix | 40% Standard, 45% Premium SOF, 15% Family Premium |
| Blended ARPU | Rs. 874/month |
| Extensive-user AI + platform variable cost | Rs. 380/subscriber/month |
| Payment gateway fee assumption | 2.5% of revenue |
| Marketing and growth spend | 12% of revenue |
| Fixed platform/tools cost | Rs. 10,000/month |

## Unit Economics

| Unit metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Blended monthly ARPU | Rs. 874 |
| AI + platform variable cost | Rs. 380 |
| Payment fee per subscriber | Rs. 22 |
| Marketing allocation per subscriber | Rs. 105 |
| Contribution before support/fixed cost | Rs. 367/subscriber/month |

The business becomes attractive once paid subscribers cross roughly 250 active accounts, assuming the current ARPU and cost structure.

## Key Recommendations

1. Keep Premium SOF at or above Rs. 999/month unless usage limits are reduced.
2. Keep Family Premium carefully capped because two children can increase token and image usage.
3. Add monthly image-generation limits by plan; image generation is the biggest variable-cost risk.
4. Track OCR/photo-upload cost separately so cost reporting is complete.
5. Maintain admin-configurable pricing and discounts, but protect contribution margin with usage limits.
6. Build a monthly finance dashboard showing revenue, AI cost, active subscribers, ARPU, and margin.

## Business Conclusion

At 100 paid subscribers, the product is still in validation mode and will likely require founder funding. At 500 paid subscribers, the business can recover the initial investment within year 1 and generate a positive ROI. At 1,000 paid subscribers, the model becomes meaningfully profitable, provided AI usage and image generation remain controlled.
