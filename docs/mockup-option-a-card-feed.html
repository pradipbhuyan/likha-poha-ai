Simple Explanation
About the story

Papa's Spectacles is a short story about a family who searches for Papa's missing glasses. It is a funny, warm story that shows how families work together to solve everyday problems.

Characters: Papa, Mama, and their children. Each person searches in a different place. In the end, the spectacles are found in a surprising spot!

🧪
Story Extract
Read this part carefully

Here is how the story begins:

"Papa could not find his spectacles anywhere. He looked on the table, under the sofa, and even inside the refrigerator — but they were nowhere to be found!"

The whole family joins the search. Mama checks the kitchen. The children look in the garden. Finally, little Ria finds them sitting right on top of Papa's head!

📖
New Words
Vocabulary from the story

  • misplacedlost or put in the wrong place by mistake
  • relievedfeeling happy that a problem or worry is over
  • franticvery worried and rushing about in a confused way

Step-by-step
How to answer story questions

When your teacher asks "What is the main idea of the story?", follow these steps:

  • 1
    Read the question carefully. Underline the key word — here it is "main idea".
  • 2
    Think about what the whole story is about, not just one part.
  • 3
    Write a sentence that mentions the characters and the problem they solved.
  • 4
    Check your answer — does it cover the beginning, middle, and end?
⚠️
Common Mistake
Watch out for this!
⚠️
Many students write "The story is about spectacles" — this is too short! Always include who the story is about and what happened. A better answer: "The story is about a family who work together to find Papa's missing glasses."
✅
Quick Check
Try this question
📝 Answer in 1–2 sentences
