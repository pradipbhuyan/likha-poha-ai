Yes — you can absolutely add more than one child to the dashboard.

The Parent Dashboard includes a Family Learning Center that acts as a central hub where you can manage multiple children. Simply click the "+ Add Child" button within this section to add another child. Once added, you can track each child's learning progress, mock test performance, and weak areas from one clean, unified dashboard.

The Parent Dashboard in LikhaPoha AI acts as a central Family Learning Center with the following features:

  • Account Management — Add multiple children and invite co-parents to share dashboard access.
  • Comprehensive Tracking — Real-time overview of daily learning progress, mock test performance, score trends, and AI usage.
  • Weak Area Alerts — Automatically highlights topics where the child is struggling so parents know exactly where to step in.
  • Actionable Suggestions — AI-powered next steps based on performance, such as recommending harder practice or Olympiad-style questions if the student is doing well.
  • Subscription Control — View, compare, and manage subscription plans and payment options.

Navigate to the Family Learning Center section within your Parent Dashboard. Just below the section showing the current number of parents and children on your account, you will see an "+ Invite Parent" button next to the "+ Add Child" button. Click it to send the invitation.

LikhaPoha AI identifies weak areas through smart evaluation based on ongoing test performance. When a student takes chapter-wise mock tests, the platform instantly scores each answer and transforms the results into a targeted revision plan.

The AI continuously tracks mock test history, average scores, and subject-specific performance over time. It then automatically flags the specific topics where the student is struggling and surfaces these as weak area alerts in two key places:

  • Student Dashboard — Acts as a daily starting point, showing the child exactly what to study next.
  • Parent Dashboard — Gives parents a clear view of weak areas with actionable suggestions so they know exactly where to step in and help.

The Analytics Dashboard turns past test performance into a targeted, actionable revision plan. Key features include:

  • Score Trend Graphs — A visual timeline mapping mock test performance over time. Students can see whether their average scores are improving with practice.
  • Subject Performance Heatmaps — A bar chart showing average scores across different subjects, instantly highlighting which subjects need more focused revision.
  • Automatic Weak Area Alerts — The AI flags specific topics where the student is struggling based on continuous analysis of test results.
  • Guided Daily Starting Point — These insights combine to show students exactly what to study next, removing the guesswork from daily revision.

Score trend graphs provide a clear visual timeline of your mock test performance over time. By continuously mapping your test results, they let you track your overall learning patterns at a glance.

You can easily see whether your average scores are improving as you practice — helping you gauge if your current revision efforts are working or if you need to shift focus to different subjects or topics.

Yes — every AI lesson on LikhaPoha AI is explicitly based on specific NCERT chapters. Lessons are grounded directly in uploaded NCERT textbooks, not generic internet content.

When setting up a lesson, students select their Grade, Subject, and exact Chapter (for example, "Structure of the Atom" for Grade 9 Science). The AI then breaks that chapter into a self-paced learning path with multiple steps — concept introduction, core explanation, worked examples, exam-style problems, and revision — all strictly following the CBSE textbook.

The Mock Test Studio lets students create highly customizable, exam-style practice tests. Available formats include class tests, mid-terms, and full mock tests across all grades.

When creating a test, students can customize:

  • Subject and Chapter — Select a specific subject and narrow down to the exact chapter or section.
  • Test and Exam Type — Choose formats like "CBSE Exam Mock Test" or "Class Test".
  • Difficulty — Adjust the challenge level (Easy, Medium, Hard).
  • Length and Time — Define exactly how many questions to include and set a custom timer.
  • Scoring Rules — Toggle negative marking to simulate real exam conditions.

Once completed, timed tests provide instant scoring and a detailed review that transforms results into a guided revision plan.

The Learn More library offers a curated selection of free learning resources hand-picked for each specific chapter — helping students strengthen their understanding beyond the core AI lessons.

By selecting their Grade, Subject, and specific Chapter, students can access:

  • NCERT articles
  • Topic-specific YouTube links
  • Open references and supplementary reading

These materials let students watch videos and read supplementary content tailored exactly to what they are studying.

LikhaPoha AI supports Class 5 to 10 for CBSE across all core subjects — Science, Maths, English, Social Science, and Hindi. State Board content can be uploaded by admins for custom deployments.
