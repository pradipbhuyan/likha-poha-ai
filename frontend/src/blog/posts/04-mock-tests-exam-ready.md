There's a big difference between understanding a topic and being able to answer questions about it under exam conditions. Students who only study without practising often find themselves unable to recall or apply what they've learned when it matters most.

Mock tests close that gap. Here's why — and how Likha Poha AI's Mock Tests feature is designed specifically for CBSE students.

## Why Studying Alone Is Not Enough

When you read a chapter, your brain absorbs information passively. You recognise concepts when you see them. But exams require you to *retrieve* that information from memory under time pressure — that's a completely different skill.

Research in learning science shows that **retrieval practice** (answering questions) is significantly more effective than re-reading for long-term memory. Every time you recall an answer from memory, you strengthen that neural pathway.

Mock tests force retrieval. That's why students who practise with regular tests consistently outperform those who only revise their notes.

## Why Variety of Questions Matters

CBSE exams do not ask the same question twice. The board deliberately tests the same concept in different ways:

- **Direct recall** — "What is Newton's First Law?"
- **Application** — "A book is lying on a table. What forces are acting on it?"
- **Diagram-based** — "Label the parts of the human heart"
- **Calculation** — "A body of mass 5 kg accelerates at 2 m/s². Find the force."
- **Assertion-Reason** — "Assertion: Plants make their own food. Reason: Plants are autotrophs."
- **Case-based** — Paragraph describing a scenario, followed by 4 questions

If you've only practised one type, you'll be caught off guard by the others.

Students who have done extensive mock test practice walk into exams with a significant advantage: familiarity. The question formats don't surprise them. The time pressure feels manageable. They've seen enough variety that nothing feels completely new.
