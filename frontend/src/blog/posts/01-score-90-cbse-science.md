Scoring 90+ in CBSE Class 10 Science is absolutely achievable with the right preparation strategy. Here's a chapter-by-chapter approach that has helped thousands of students.

## Understand the Exam Pattern First

The CBSE Class 10 Science paper is 80 marks (3 hours). The distribution is:
- **Chemical Substances** (25 marks) — Chapters 1–4
- **World of Living** (23 marks) — Chapters 6, 7, 8, 9
- **Natural Phenomena** (12 marks) — Chapters 10, 11
- **Effects of Current** (13 marks) — Chapters 12, 13
- **Natural Resources** (7 marks) — Chapter 16

## High-Priority Chapters (Focus Here First)

### 1. Chemical Reactions and Equations
- Learn all reaction types: combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement, oxidation/reduction
- Memorise key chemical equations — these appear directly in exams
- Practice balancing equations daily for 10 minutes

### 2. Life Processes
- Draw and label diagrams: nutrition, respiration, transportation, excretion
- Understand the difference between autotrophic and heterotrophic nutrition
- NCERT diagrams appear exactly in exams — memorise them

### 3. Light — Reflection and Refraction
- Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
- Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v – 1/u
- Practice ray diagrams for concave mirror, convex mirror, concave lens, convex lens

### 4. Electricity
- Ohm's Law: V = IR
- Series and parallel combinations
- Calculate equivalent resistance for 5-6 resistor problems daily

## Revision Strategy (Last 30 Days)

**Week 1–2:** Complete all NCERT exercises. Do not skip a single question.

**Week 3:** Solve last 5 years' CBSE papers. Identify recurring question patterns.

**Week 4:** Focus on weak chapters. Use the LikhaPoha AI doubt-solving tool for any concept you're unsure about.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not writing units** — lose 1 mark per calculation
- **Skipping diagram labels** — each missing label costs half a mark
- **Memorising without understanding** — CBSE increasingly tests application
