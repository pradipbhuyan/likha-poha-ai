Most parents face the same evening question: "Did you study today?" And most children say yes — whether it's true or not.

The real question is not whether your child studied, but **what** they studied, **how well** they understood it, and **where** they need more help.

Likha Poha AI's Parent Dashboard answers all three.

## What the Parent Dashboard Shows You

When you log in as a parent, you see a real-time summary of your child's learning activity:

### 1. Recent Test Scores
Every mock test your child takes is recorded with the subject, chapter, date, and score. You can see at a glance:
- Which chapters they've attempted
- Whether scores are improving over time
- Which subjects need more practice

### 2. Weak Area Alerts
This is the most powerful feature. When a child scores below a threshold on a chapter test, the system automatically flags it as a weak area. You'll see:
- Subject and chapter name
- Number of attempts
- Best score so far

This tells you exactly what to focus on before the next exam — without any guesswork.

### 3. Score Trend Charts
Visual graphs show how your child's average score has changed over time. You can see whether revision is working or whether a subject is being neglected.

### 4. Subject Performance
A breakdown by subject shows average scores across all tests. This is especially useful for identifying which subject needs a tutor or extra attention.

## How to Use the Dashboard Effectively

**Every Sunday:** Check the weak area alerts. Sit with your child for 15 minutes and review the flagged chapters together.

**Before exams:** Look at the score trend for each subject. If Science scores are declining, that's where to focus.

**After report cards:** Compare the dashboard predictions with actual grades. You'll notice the weak area alerts often predict exactly which subjects had lower marks.

## For Parents with Multiple Children

The Family Plan supports two children under one account. You can switch between children in the dashboard and track each child's progress separately. Both parents can log in and view the same dashboard — so both Mum and Dad stay in the loop.
